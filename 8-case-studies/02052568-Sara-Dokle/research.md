•Introduction
Artificial intelligence has become one of the most talked about topics in the tech industry recently. Many companies are experimenting with AI tools to improve the way they develop software.

•How companies use AI
AI is playing an increasingly important role in the software development industry. Many large technology companies are investing in AI systems to improve productivity, reduce costs, and provide better quality services to users. Some of the most well known examples are GitHub Copilot, Shopify, and Duolingo, which have integrated AI in various ways into their work processes.
GitHub Copilot uses advanced artificial intelligence models to assist programmers when writing code. It suggests functions, algorithms, and code snippets in real time, making the programming process faster and more efficient.
Shopify uses AI to help online businesses create product descriptions, analyze customer behavior, and optimize sales.
On the other hand, Duolingo uses AI to personalize language learning, adapting exercises according to the level and performance of each user.

•Workflow Changes
The integration of AI has significantly changed the way development teams work. Tasks that were previously time consuming, such as writing basic code, analyzing data, or creating content, can now be completed faster with the help of AI. This allows developers to focus on solving more complex problems and improving products.

•Advantages
-Increases team productivity.
-Reduces project development time.
-Automates routine and repetitive tasks.
-Improves data analysis and decision-making.
-Provides more personalized experiences for users.

•Limitations
Although AI brings many benefits to users, it is not perfect. The generated suggestions may contain errors or be inappropriate for specific situations. Furthermore, AI does not have the ability to fully understand the business context or the specific requirements of a project.

•Challenges
One of the main challenges is verifying the results produced by AI. Development teams must continuously review the code and generated content to ensure quality and security. Companies must also address issues such as data privacy, cybersecurity, and the ethical use of artificial intelligence.

•Conclusion
Artificial intelligence is transforming the way companies develop and maintain their software. Examples such as GitHub Copilot, Shopify, and Duolingo show that AI can improve productivity, automate routine tasks, and provide more personalized experiences for users. However, the use of AI also brings challenges, such as the need for human control and managing potential risks. In the future, collaboration between developers and AI systems is expected to become an important part of software engineering. In my opinion, AI will continue to become an important part of programmers' work, but technical knowledge and the ability to solve problems will always remain necessary.
